Who We Are

A Legacy of Quality & Innovation

Established in the year 2016, Vivek Polymers has emerged as a prominent manufacturer and supplier of all types of engineering polymers.

Under the adept leadership of Mr. Dilip Mishra, we have established a strong presence in the market. His visionary approach and unwavering motivation have enabled us to consistently meet and exceed our clients' expectations with efficiency.

Our firm has become one of the fastest growing Plastic Granules manufacturers and suppliers of plastic raw material including Polyethylene Granules, Polypropylene Granules, PPCP Granules, ABS Granules, Polycarbonate Granules, TPE Granules, Nylon Granules, PMMA Granules, SAN Granules and reprocessed plastic.
